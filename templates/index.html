Entity-Level Splitting
"The split was performed at the Source 1 entity level to prevent data leakage."
All candidate pairs for a given S1 entity reside exclusively in training or validation — never both. This ensures the model is evaluated on truly unseen businesses.
Candidate Generation (Blocking)
Country-exact blocking + multi-metric fuzzy similarity threshold
Only pairs passing similarity thresholds (name or address ≥ 50% token sort ratio) advance to full feature extraction. This eliminates computationally infeasible pairs while preserving high recall.
Primary Evaluation Metric
F0.5
Precision-Weighted Metric
"F0.5 gives greater importance to precision, which is important for avoiding incorrect business merges."

In enterprise entity resolution, a false merge (incorrectly linking two distinct legal entities) carries significantly higher operational and compliance risk than a false non-merge.
